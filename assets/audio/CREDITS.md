# Audio credits

## westminster.mp3
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons — *File:Westminster-and-one-o-clock.ogg*
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Westminster-and-one-o-clock.ogg>
- **Recording:** the Westminster chime + one o'clock strike of a rod-gong
  clockwork made by Kieninger (Germany), self-recorded by Wikimedia user *Wikipit* (2005).
- **License:** Public Domain (released worldwide by the copyright holder).
- **Processing:** transcoded from Ogg Vorbis to MP3 with ffmpeg; played back
  sliced into per-quarter phrases and a repeated strike segment.

## Synthesized (no audio file)
- **Whittington** and **St. Michael** chimes, the **hour strike** for those
  tunes, and the **per-second escapement tick** are generated in-browser with
  the Web Audio API — no freely-licensed recordings of these were available.
