# Audio credits

## bells/*.mp3 — chime bells (all three tunes)
Tubular-bell note samples used to play the Westminster, Whittington, and
St. Michael chimes note-by-note with full natural decay. See
[`bells/CREDITS.md`](bells/CREDITS.md). License: **CC0 1.0** (public domain),
FreePats / Versilian Community Sample Library.

## westminster.mp3 — hour strike
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons — *File:Westminster-and-one-o-clock.ogg*
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Westminster-and-one-o-clock.ogg>
- **Recording:** a rod-gong clockwork made by Kieninger (Germany), self-recorded
  by Wikimedia user *Wikipit* (2005).
- **License:** Public Domain (released worldwide by the copyright holder).
- **Use:** the hour **strike** is the gong segment of this recording (it rings
  out naturally). The chime melody itself is now played from the CC0 tubular
  bells above, not from this recording.
- **Processing:** transcoded from Ogg Vorbis to MP3 with ffmpeg.

## Synthesized (no audio file)
- The **per-second escapement tick** is generated in-browser with the Web Audio
  API. A synthesized bell remains as a fallback if a sample fails to load.
