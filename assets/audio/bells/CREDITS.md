# Tubular bell samples — credits

These per-note tubular-bell samples drive all three chime melodies
(Westminster, Whittington, St. Michael). Each note is a one-shot that rings its
full natural decay; the clock plays them note-by-note (pitch-shifting the
nearest sample by ≤1 semitone) so chimes fade out naturally.

- **Source:** FreePats "Tubular Bells" — <https://freepats.zenvoid.org/ChromaticPercussion/tubular-bells.html>
  (built from the Versilian Community Sample Library by Versilian Studios).
- **License:** Creative Commons **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication — no
  attribution required; credited here anyway).
- **Processing:** the small WAV pack's one-shots were downmixed to mono, trimmed
  to ~7 s with a short tail fade, and encoded to MP3 with ffmpeg.
- **Files:** `C4 D4 E4 Fs4 Gs4 As4 C5 D5 E5 .mp3` (`s` = sharp).
